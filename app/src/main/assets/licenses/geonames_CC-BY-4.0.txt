GeoNames place data

The list of towns and cities in places/places.tsv (names, countries, positions and populations,
for places of 15,000 people or more) is derived from the GeoNames gazetteer, cities15000 extract.

  Source:  https://www.geonames.org/
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made: reduced to six fields per place, coordinates rounded to two decimals, sorted by name.
