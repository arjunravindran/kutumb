# Family words to check: Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil

Kutumb names a relationship with the word a family would use. Hindi's words were checked; the four
languages below are a **first draft by an AI and have not been read by anyone who speaks them**.

**How to help.** Find your language's column. For each row, the first column says who the person is
(for example *father's younger brother*). If the word in your language is what a family would say, leave
it. If it is wrong, awkward or only used in some regions, write the word you would use in its place.
If your language has two words where the draft has one (or one where it has two), say so; that matters
more than spelling. Hindi is shown only for comparison.

Some things to know before you start:

- A word repeated down a column is on purpose where the language really uses one word for several people
  (Marathi आजोबा is both grandfathers). Say if that is wrong.
- Tamil and Telugu use short descriptions for cousins and step-relatives where I was not sure of a single word.
- *Elder* and *younger* matter in some places (father's brothers in Hindi, Bengali, Telugu and Tamil; husband's
  brothers). The app asks for birth years to choose between them and shows a descriptive phrase when it
  cannot tell.
