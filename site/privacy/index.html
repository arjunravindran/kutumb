Kutumb privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · applies to Kutumb for Android, the desktop app and this website

We collect no data. We share no data. Ever. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of ours that your information could reach.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter, including names, dates, relationships, photographs, notes and messages, is stored only on your phone or computer. The developer has no way to see it, because the app never sends it to the developer. We run no servers that receive it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, telemetry or third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not know who uses Kutumb, how often, or what is in anyone's tree.

What we share

Nothing. We have nothing to share, so we do not sell, rent, trade or disclose any information to anyone, for any reason, including advertising, research or legal requests: we hold no data to hand over.

Things you can choose to do, and what they involve

These happen only when you start them. Each moves information to someone you picked, never to us.

  • Exporting or sharing a file. A .ftree file or a family book PDF goes wherever you send it, through the share sheet or a file you save.
  • Nearby sharing. Phones on the same Wi-Fi network exchange a tree directly with each other. Nothing passes through the internet or through us.
  • Sync with family over the internet (off by default). If you turn this on, the app connects to a public relay server (by default nos.lol, or the ones you choose under Advanced) and exchanges messages that are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they leave it. A relay's operator can see that a message passed through and the network address it came from, but not its content. Relays are run by third parties, not by us, and have their own policies. Choose ones you trust, or leave sync off, in which case the app contacts no server at all. Until you turn sync on, no relay is contacted.

The viewer on this website

The viewer opens a .ftree file in your browser. The file is read and drawn on your device and is never uploaded: there is no server of ours for it to reach. If you leave Keep my tree on this device ticked, the viewer also saves a copy of the tree in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) so that it opens straight away next time, and so it works without a network once it has been installed to your home screen. That copy stays in this browser on this device. Pressing Close, or unticking the box, deletes it; clearing the site's data in your browser settings does too. The typefaces are served from this site too, so visiting the page does not contact Google or any other third party.

Permissions

  • Contacts, only if you choose Add people from my contacts. Kutumb reads names and birthdays from your contacts on your phone, shows them to you to tick, and copies only the people you pick into your tree. Phone numbers, addresses and emails are never read, and nothing about your contacts is sent anywhere.
  • Camera, only to scan a pairing code. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Notifications, for birthday reminders and incoming messages, generated on your phone.
  • Internet and network state, for nearby sharing and for sync, if you turn it on.
  • Start at boot, to restore reminders after a restart.

Photographs and files are read only when you choose them, using the system picker.

Children

We collect nothing from anyone, including children. The app does not ask for an age and has no account to create.

Deleting your data

Delete a person or a whole tree inside the app, or uninstall it, and the data is gone from the device. For the website viewer, press Close or clear the site's data in your browser. There is nothing on our side to delete. Copies you exported or shared stay with whoever received them.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changed in a way that affected this policy, the new policy would be published here before that release, with a new date. The promise at the top will not be weakened quietly.

Contact

Questions: open an issue. The source code is public, so every claim above can be checked.
